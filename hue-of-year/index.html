Hue of Year

A diary for the end of the day. Each evening you log how it went, your energy and one small win. Every day becomes a colour, and over months a year of moods takes shape on a single grid.

Get it on the App Store

At a glance

Made for
iPhone. There is no Android version.
Status
Live on the App Store
Your entries
Stay on your phone. No account needed. Deleting the app deletes them.
Pro
An optional upgrade that unlocks extra features

What it does

Evening check-in
Log your mood, your energy and one win from the day. Optionally set one task for tomorrow.
A year in colour
Thriving is green, okay is amber, struggling is red. Day by day, the colours fill a grid of the whole year.
Prism
The forward-looking view, where you set tomorrow’s task.
Echo
The backward-looking view: mood by weekday, energy, task completion, and how you bounce back after a hard day.
Task follow-up
The next evening starts by asking whether you did it. If not, note why and carry it forward.
Widgets
Home and lock screen widgets show the year grid, today’s mood and today’s task.
Evening reminder
An optional daily nudge, 9 PM by default, skipped if you have already logged.
