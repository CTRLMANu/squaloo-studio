Hue of Year

No. 02

AI usage → CO2

In progress

I used about 1.3 billion tokens over July and part of August, and wanted to know what that costs in CO₂. So I'm building a small, free tool that estimates it from the exports you can already download, without installing anything.

Read the full entry

What it will do
ReadsYour ChatGPT and Claude.ai data exports, Cursor's usage CSV, and Claude Code's local logs
ShowsTokens by model and type, and CO₂ as a low, middle and high estimate
ExplainsEvery assumption with its source, which you can adjust
SuggestsWays to cut your footprint first, then where to offset the rest
RunsEntirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.
LicenceFree and open source (MIT)

A note from the compiler

I'm Manuel. I build things on the side, partly as a creative outlet, partly because I need to be making something. This is where they live: small, careful tools that show people something about themselves they'd otherwise miss, like a year of moods or the footprint of their AI use.

That second one started with my own: 1.3 billion tokens over July and part of August. Two entries so far. More as they ship.
