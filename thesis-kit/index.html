thesis-kit

A Cursor workspace for writing a thesis from sources you checked yourself. You open the folder, and it finds papers, checks them, and drafts one section at a time. It only cites papers you have opened and confirmed.

Get it on GitHub

What it does

OpensYou clone the repository and open the folder in Cursor. No coding.
ChecksEvery paper against Crossref and DataCite. It can be cited only after you confirm you read it.
DraftsOne section at a time, from those papers. Anything it cannot back up is marked [CHECK].
Hands offA Word file you keep editing, then supervisor comments brought back in as a list.
RecordsAn optional log of what the AI did, which becomes your AI declaration.
LicenceFree and open source (MIT)

At a glance

Status
Live on GitHub. You can use it now. Small parts are still being tightened.
You write
The thesis stays yours. The kit drafts one section, and you rewrite it in your own words.
Your files
A thesis folder on your computer. An online backup is optional.
Alongside
Consensus, Semantic Scholar, ResearchRabbit, Zotero, and Gemini Notebook. All optional.
