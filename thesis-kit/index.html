thesis-kit

A Cursor workspace for writing a thesis from sources you checked yourself. You clone it, open the folder, and it already knows the next step: the question, the papers you kept, then one section at a time.

See the repository

The plan

What it will do
OpensYou clone the repository and open that folder in Cursor
Holdsquestion.md, memo.md, sources.md, outline.md, and an empty chapters folder
Walks throughA five-step guide, plus skills for a research memo, one literature-review section, a paraphrase, citations, and supervisor comments
Stays outsideConsensus, ResearchRabbit, Zotero, NotebookLM, and an OpenPaper exposé. You open the site, then paste what you keep
WritesOne section from the papers in the folder. It marks anything unsure and stops
